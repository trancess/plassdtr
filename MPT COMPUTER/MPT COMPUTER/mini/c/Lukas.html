Lukas

A legendary Sacred Beast that can take the form of a rambunctious young man ” Lukas is a recently revived from inside the Imperial Sanctuary. Endowed with a special bloodline that allows him to take human form, he was once befriended by Emperor Gaius and fought alongside him to create the glorious Moniyan Empire. After a millennium-long slumber, he was awakened by Silvanna and vowed to herald a new era for the Empire at her side. Beneath his brazen and rambunctious appearance lies a pure and earnest heart. For Lukas, fighting is not only his duty but the instinctive expression of his spirit. He firmly believes he will create something as brilliant as the dawning sun to illuminate every corner of the Moniyan Empire and rewrite the legend of the Beast of Light.

Fun Fact: Lukas is one of the newer heroes added to Mobile Legends. He can transform into a powerful beast-like form, giving him enhanced abilities. His gameplay revolves around timing his transformation for maximum impact in team fights. Lukas excels at chasing enemies thanks to his mobility and crowd control. Many players compare his transformation mechanic to heroes from action RPGs because it dramatically changes his fighting style.
