Alice

Upon her throne in the Sanguine Lair, with blood straining the hem of her dress, Alice looked down at those who knelt before her and asked with a slight smile "What is love?" To this question, the young Alice would have answered: Love is the protection and sense of worth bestowed by her father. Alice was the illegitimate daughter of a nobleman and a lowly dancer but with a powerful affinity to dark magic. Her mother always told her that their beautiful lifestyle was proof that they were loved. Her father would often take young Alice to see the family's sacred treasure, the Crown of Antalus, telling her the tales of the family's past glory and how she would help restore their greatness. Alice understood from an early age that being loved meant her role was to be the family's knife in the shadow. Using her gift for dark magic, Alice moved through the southern battlefields, manipulating hearts and minds to eliminate obstacles for the Antalus Family. Her half-brothers' contempt for her illegitimate status was mere static in the symphony of love. Alice held her head high and ignored their petty grievances with arrogant pride. She had always loved her father and the family despite everything. Even when she was captured and tortured by the Monastery of Light during their final mission, Alice gritted her teeth and revealed none of the family's dark secrets. But does love truly exist? While imprisoned, a mysterious figure revealed a shocking truth to Alice: her whole life, she had been nothing but a pawn. Alice sneered in response, dismissing it as just another one of the Enemy's ploys. The figure shook their head, handed a mysterious orb to Alice, and unlocked her door cell. Covered in wounds, Alice made her way home only to overhear a conversation between her father and the elders. Her failure and capture had been a calculated deal between the Antalus family and the Monastery of Light, in a bid to restore the family's former glory. And love? Alice looked at her father and only saw only cold eyes. Love never existed - from the beginning, there had only been her worth, desires, and lies. Her birth was permitted because of her talent; her freedom granted because she had use to the family. Love was a sweet poison that family showed its true nature. The Family had long prepared magic to suppress Alice. They watched coldly as she was pinned to the ground, struggling like a wounded animal; they had already arranged for her to be taken back by the Monastery of Light. But at the peak of her despair, the orb in Alice's possession began to glow. She knew that mysterious figure might be another player in the Antalus Family trying to use her, but what did it matter? Alice laughed as despair and hatred consumed her body. The power of the Abyss transformed her blood completely - crimson wings burst from her flesh as demonic energy engulfed the entire family. Alice crouched down and whispered the truth into her dying father's ear: Even if they handed her to the Monastery of Light, the Antalus Family would never receive what was promised. In the eyes of those above, her father was a pawn himself. The Antalus Family's dream of reclaiming their former glory, like Alice's desperate yearning for love, was nothing but a deluded fantasy. Her father drew his last breath amid Alice's bloodcurdling laughter. One day, Beatrix's parents returned from the far west, bringing along with them a range of advanced gadgets from Eruditio to give her. Beatrix had long grown tired of the mundane and uninteresting toys that surrounded her, and so she began to obsess over these novelties. They sparked within her a deep interest in technology itself. In the several years that followed, Morgan would regularly shuttle between Eruditio and Castle Gorge to bring Beatrix the latest inventions, which proved to be an extremely expensive endeavor. As she tirelessly studied them, Beatrix began to nurture within herself an incredible natural talent for science. She digested many complex mechanical concepts and structures as if they were child's play. Before long, she had built her own laboratory within the castle grounds and seriously researched various technologies and weaponry.

Fun Fact: Alice is known as the "Queen of Blood" and is one of the oldest heroes in the game. She can gain permanent HP by collecting Blood Orbs from minions and enemy heroes. Alice has been revamped multiple times since Mobile Legends was released. Her ultimate, Blood Ode, drains enemies' HP while healing herself, making her very hard to kill in team fights.
