Thamuz

The lord of the Fire Demons who seeks to reignite the flames of the Abyss through war and violence. ” In the deepest depths of the Abyss, molten magma churns at the birthplace of the formidable Fire Demons. Among them was Thamuz, who rose to become the mightiest Fire Demon and their leader. With his overwhelming strength and blazing temper, he controls the depths of the Abyss with an iron first. Now, as the corruption seeping through the Abyss threatens to wipe out his race, Thamuz seeks to ascend to godhood and crush fate with his own hands. To this end, he and his Fire Demons will burn everything to ash, until nothing is left. The Sun Below “ You must never allow the Heart of Lava to become cold and harden. ” Thamuz still remembered the words of the former Lord of Lava. Thamuz was just a general back then — when the Sovereign of the Abyss ordered them to go to war, he forged his scythes from obsidian and lava, and reaped the battlefield with his blazing flames. However, the former lord saw in him not just strength that surpassed all others, but also wisdom rare among their kind. After the sovereign issued another call to arms, the former lord summoned Thamuz once more to the birthplace of the Fire Demons. This time, however, it wasn't to send him off to battle — it was to keep him from it.

Fun Fact: Thamuz, the Lord of Lava, is one of the most aggressive fighters in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. Known for his incredible sustain and base damage, he actually has some of the most unique lore and mechanics in the game.
